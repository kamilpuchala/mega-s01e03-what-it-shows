  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants write the day first, so day and month swap: “Booked for 2 December. The invite says 12 February.” This is what #4471 describes, and what its queued fix covers.
  • hint: tenants on booking-v3 who write the day first see the hint MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. Their people type month first, as told, and the form reads the date day first. The result also looks like a swap, but the fix is the form’s hint, not the parser. The #4471 page already shows this group as “no ticket of its own”.
  • one-day-move: dates move by one day, or differ by where the person is: “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” No day and month swap. This is a time zone problem, and the parser fix will not touch it. The desk noted these reports (desk-notes.json), but they were only a footnote under parser.

How the reports were counted

  • Each report counts once, by id. The export repeats some reports, because its pages overlap.
  • parser and hint come from causeOf in causes.ts: the tenant’s locale and booking form decide it, not the intake tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser and disagrees often.
  • one-day-move is the desk’s own list in desk-notes.json. Each of those reports was read: none of them is a day/month swap.
  • The numbers above are built from the data when the page renders. When more reports arrive, they update.

Why one ticket hid three

Decision 0001 merges reports whose subject lines match. Subjects like “Date is wrong” or “Month and day swapped?” appear in all three groups, so the subject cannot tell them apart. Only the tenant, the booking form, and the body can.
